1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile party word game Thirty!, published by PlayPact Games under the Android package identifier games.playpact.thirty.

Thirty! does not require an account. You do not need to provide your legal name, email address, postal address or phone number to play.

2. Privacy at a glance

GAME DATATeam names, optional player names, settings and custom categories are saved locally.
NO ACCOUNTThere is no PlayPact Games login or developer-operated cloud profile.
PURCHASESGoogle Play or Apple handles payment. Thirty! receives purchase status so it can unlock content.
ADVERTISINGThe free version may show ads through Google Mobile Ads. Remove Ads or Premium disables new ad requests from Thirty!.
Your custom words and inside jokes stay in the app’s local storage and are not uploaded to PlayPact Games.

3. Data stored on your device

Thirty! saves a small amount of information so your choices remain available between sessions:

  • team names and optional player names entered during setup;
  • game and house-rule settings, selected categories and clue-giver rotation;
  • custom category names and the custom words you enter;
  • whether the first-time tutorial has been completed;
  • locally cached purchase entitlements, such as Premium, category packs or Remove Ads.

This information is stored using the operating system’s app storage. It is not sent to a PlayPact Games account or game-data server. Depending on your device and backup settings, Apple or Google may include eligible app data in a device or cloud backup.

Audio and microphone

Thirty! plays local game sounds and can trigger device haptics. It does not record microphone audio, perform speech recognition or upload conversations.

4. In-app purchases

Thirty! offers optional one-time purchases through the platform’s in-app purchase system. Google Play or Apple handles your payment credentials and financial transaction; PlayPact Games does not receive your complete card or bank details.

The app processes product identifiers, purchase state, transaction information and purchase tokens supplied by the platform so it can grant, remember and restore purchases. Google and Apple process purchase and account information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

The free version of Thirty! may show advertising through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Where required, Google’s consent tools may be used to ask for or manage advertising consent before ads are requested.

When ads are enabled, Google may process information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address (which can be used to estimate approximate location), ad interactions, app or device information and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, security and fraud-prevention purposes. The exact information processed can depend on your device, region, account settings and consent choices.

Thirty! does not sell team names, player names, custom categories or custom words to advertisers. Purchasing Remove Ads or Thirty! Premium prevents Thirty! from making new advertising requests while that entitlement is active. Google may retain information from earlier ad activity according to its own privacy policy.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. Network use and third parties

Normal offline gameplay—including teams, scores, built-in words and custom categories—does not require a PlayPact Games server.

When the app checks store ownership, opens the Store, buys an item or restores purchases, Thirty! communicates with Google Play or Apple through the platform purchase service. Those services may process device, account, diagnostic and transaction information according to their own terms.

Apart from the platform purchase services and the advertising services described above, the current version does not integrate a separate product-analytics service, crash-reporting service or social login. If those practices change, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before or alongside the relevant release.

We do not sell team names, player names, custom categories or custom words.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally saved data remains until you edit or delete it, clear Thirty!’s app storage through your device settings, or uninstall the app. Individual custom categories can be renamed, edited or deleted inside the Categories screen.

Purchase records may remain with Google or Apple for transaction, restoration, security, accounting and legal purposes according to their retention policies. Removing the app does not delete purchase records held by your app-store provider.

Because Thirty! has no developer-hosted player database, PlayPact Games cannot remotely access or delete data that exists only on your device.

8. Security

Thirty! relies on the operating system’s application sandbox to protect locally stored game data. Purchase communication is handled through the platform store. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and device access, backups and operating-system settings can affect the protection of local information.

9. Children’s privacy

Thirty! is a social party game and does not ask players to create accounts or submit contact details. Player names can be nicknames and remain locally stored. The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

An optional locked “After Dark” category pack contains adult-oriented party topics. Access to store purchases and age controls is managed through the device and platform account settings.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Names: use team names or nicknames rather than legal names.
  • Custom content: edit or delete a custom category inside Thirty!.
  • Local data: clear the app’s storage in device settings or uninstall the app.
  • Purchases: restore eligible purchases in the Store and manage transaction history through Google Play or Apple.
  • Ads: use any consent controls shown in the app or platform settings, and purchase Remove Ads or Premium to stop new ad requests from Thirty!.
  • Backups: manage device and cloud-backup preferences through your operating-system account settings.

Depending on where you live, privacy law may provide rights relating to information controlled by PlayPact Games. Contact us using the address below. For information controlled independently by Google or Apple, use that provider’s privacy controls.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Thirty!’s features, SDKs, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated whenever a revised version is published. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or store listing where appropriate.
