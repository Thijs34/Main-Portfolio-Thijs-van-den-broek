1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile arcade game CubeRush, published by PlayPact Games.

CubeRush does not require an account. You do not need to provide your name, email address, postal address, phone number or profile information to play.

2. Privacy at a glance

LOCAL GAME DATAScores, achievements, settings and control preferences are stored on your device.
NO ACCOUNTThere is no PlayPact Games login or developer-operated player database.
TILT STAYS LIVEMotion data is read only while Tilt is active. Raw readings are not stored or uploaded by CubeRush.
NO DATA SALEPlayPact Games does not sell your CubeRush gameplay or settings data.
Your high score, achievements and settings stay in the app’s local storage and are not uploaded to a PlayPact Games account.

3. Data stored on your device

CubeRush saves a small amount of information so progress and preferences remain available:

  • best score, lifetime score, run totals and achievement progress;
  • music and sound-effect volume;
  • selected control mode and tilt sensitivity;
  • locally cached Remove Ads purchase status, when available;
  • basic ad pacing state, such as run count and last interstitial time, when advertising is enabled.

This information is stored in Android application storage using Unity PlayerPrefs. CubeRush does not upload it to a PlayPact Games game-data server. Depending on your Android backup settings, Google may include eligible app data in a device or cloud backup.

4. Tilt controls and sensors

If you choose Tilt controls, CubeRush reads the phone’s motion/gravity sensor to calculate steering and calibrate a neutral angle when a run starts.

Raw readings are used in real time. CubeRush does not store a sensor history, use it for advertising or transmit it to PlayPact Games. Only your selected sensitivity is stored locally.

5. Unity engine diagnostics

CubeRush is made with Unity. The version covered by this policy may use Unity engine diagnostics for stability and performance. Depending on Unity’s platform behavior and configuration, Unity may process device and operating-system details, app version, IP address, crash or exception information, performance signals and diagnostic identifiers.

Product analytics, social login and a PlayPact Games tracking profile are not integrated in the current version. Unity handles its diagnostic information under the Unity Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising

CURRENT INTEGRATION STATUS

The app architecture supports optional rewarded Continue ads and occasional interstitial ads, but the build covered by this policy does not yet have a live third-party advertising provider connected. The Unity Editor uses a local test simulation; it is not a real ad and does not contact an ad network.

Before advertising is enabled in a distributed version, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated to name the provider and explain its data processing. Required consent controls will be shown before requesting personalized advertising.

Remove Ads, once active, will stop automatic/interstitial ad requests. Optional rewarded Continue ads may remain available because the player chooses whether to request them.

7. In-app purchases

CURRENT INTEGRATION STATUS

CubeRush includes a planned one-time Remove Ads option, but the current player build does not yet have a live store provider connected.

When enabled, Google Play will handle payment credentials and the financial transaction. PlayPact Games will not receive complete card or bank details. CubeRush will process the product identifier and purchase status supplied by Google Play to grant, remember and restore the purchase. See the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear CubeRush’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Because CubeRush has no PlayPact Games player database, PlayPact Games cannot remotely access or delete data that exists only on your device.

Unity diagnostics and future Google purchase records are retained according to those providers’ policies and legal obligations. Uninstalling CubeRush does not necessarily delete records held independently by a platform provider.

9. Security

CubeRush relies on Android’s application sandbox to protect locally stored game data. No storage or transmission method is completely secure; device access, backups and operating-system settings can affect local information.

10. Children’s privacy

CubeRush does not ask players to create an account or submit contact details. PlayPact Games does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a CubeRush account or player database.

If advertising or another third-party service is added, its configuration and the game’s intended-audience classification will be reviewed before release, and this policy will be updated where necessary.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Local data: clear CubeRush storage in Android settings or uninstall it.
  • Tilt: select Drag or Tap instead; raw tilt readings are not retained by CubeRush.
  • Ads: do not request an optional rewarded Continue ad. Further controls will be added if required when live ads are introduced.
  • Purchases: manage transaction history and eligible restoration through Google Play once active.
  • Backups: manage app-data backup preferences in Google/Android settings.

Depending on where you live, privacy law may provide rights concerning personal information controlled by PlayPact Games. Contact us below. For data controlled independently by Unity or Google, use that provider’s privacy controls.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when CubeRush’s features, SDKs, legal requirements or data practices change. The date at the top will be updated whenever a revision is published. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or store listing.
